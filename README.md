# 📊 InsightIQ AI

### Autonomous Business Intelligence & Decision Support Platform

InsightIQ AI is an AI-powered analytics platform that automatically analyzes business datasets, generates KPIs, creates insights, performs forecasting, and produces executive-level reports.

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights

- 📂 Automatic dataset understanding
- 🧠 AI-generated business insights
- 📊 Dynamic KPI generation
- 📈 Forecasting for business metrics
- 📄 Automated executive reports
- 🔍 Natural language data exploration

---

## 📁 Supported Datasets

InsightIQ AI currently supports:

| Dataset Type | Example Dataset | Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | Store Sales / Online Orders | Revenue analysis, sales trends, forecasting |
| Ride Analytics | Uber Ride Bookings | Booking trends, revenue, distance analysis |
| Retail | Walmart Sales | Weekly sales forecasting, external factor analysis |
| Fraud Detection | PaySim Transactions | Transaction analysis and fraud patterns |
| HR Analytics | Employee datasets | Workforce insights and performance metrics |

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Business Dashboard
Automatically generates relevant KPIs based on the uploaded dataset.

### 🤖 AI Business Analyst
Provides business insights, trends, risks, and recommendations using AI.

### 📈 Forecasting Engine
Predicts future business metrics such as:

- Revenue
- Weekly Sales
- Booking Value
- Transaction Volume

### 📄 Executive Report
Creates leadership-ready summaries combining analytics and forecasts.
